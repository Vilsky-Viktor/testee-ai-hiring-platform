# Candidate notice templates

Version of 2026-11-07. Arcolabs OÜ (registry code 17587452), Sepapaja tn 6, 15551 Tallinn, Estonia; hello@testee.ai.

For companies to adapt and send **before** inviting candidates to a testee interview. Replace
everything in [brackets]. The notice is yours to give as the employer: these templates cover what
testee does, and you add how you use the results. In the EU, telling candidates that a high-risk
AI system is used to assess them is one of your duties under the AI Act from 2027-12-02; the
general notice (section 2) does it. Illinois, Colorado and Connecticut candidates get the general
notice with the state wording in sections 4, 5 and 6 added.

## 1. New York City (Local Law 144, automated employment decision tool)

Local Law 144 applies when an employer or employment agency uses an automated employment decision
tool to substantially assist hiring decisions in New York City: for a job at an office there (at
least part time), a fully remote job tied to one, or through an agency located there. The notice
goes to candidates who live in the city. Whether testee is such a tool depends on how you use it. The rules require, among other things: a bias audit within
one year before use with a public summary; a notice at least **10 business days** before use (in
the job posting, on the careers site, or by mail or email); and information on how to request an
alternative selection process or accommodation.

Under Local Law 144 the employer or employment agency must make sure a bias audit exists and gives
the notice: testee doesn't do it for you. You can rely on an audit built on several employers' data
only if your own data is in it or you haven't used the tool yet. testee gives you the candidates' results for your auditor
(the owner's bias-audit export, the API, PDF reports), the summary of its own independent bias audit once published at
testee.ai/documents, and this template. Don't use testee for candidates in New York City before
your audit summary is published (see testee's instructions for companies).

> **Notice of use of an automated employment decision tool**
>
> [Company] uses **testee**, an automated employment decision tool, to help assess candidates for
> the position of [job title]. We are giving you this notice at least 10 business days before you
> will be assessed.
>
> **What it assesses.** A timed multiple-choice test of job knowledge in these areas, taken from
> the job's requirements: [list topics]. Each question has its own time limit, on average [N]
> seconds: shorter questions get less, longer ones and code more. Your score is the percentage
> of points earned: each question is worth one point, and on a "select all that apply" question
> you earn part of it for each right option ticked, and nothing if you tick a wrong one. The tool
> also records how long each answer took, when you left the test page or copied text during it,
> and answers given too fast to have read the question. It also shows [Company] when your answer
> times or your right and wrong answers don't follow the questions' length and difficulty as other
> candidates' do (for example, about the same time on every question), with questions to ask you
> about in an interview; it never decides on its own. Before you start, you confirm that you'll
> answer on your own, without AI tools or other people, and when you did is recorded. [Company]
> may give you extra time, which is recorded without a reason.
>
> **How it is used.** Your score is compared with a passing grade of [N]% and with other
> candidates' scores. [Name or role at Company] reviews every result before any decision, together
> with [your application / interviews]. The tool does not make the hiring decision.
>
> **Data.** The tool uses your email address, your name (from your application or your sign-in),
> the account id from your sign-in, and your answers in the test, with how long each took, page
> leaves, copy attempts, answers too fast to have read the question, when you confirmed answering
> on your own, and any extra time you were given. The questions were written with AI from the job description,
> [on topics [Company] chose / on topics drawn from it]. Your test data is kept by testee for 12 months after your invitation, then
> deleted. [Company's own retention policy: ...]. More information about the data collected, its
> source and retention policy is available on request at [contact], within 30 days of a written
> request.
>
> **Bias audit.** A summary of the most recent bias audit of this tool is available at [link to
> your bias audit summary].
>
> **Alternative process or accommodation.** You may request an alternative selection process or an
> accommodation (for example extra time) by writing to [contact] by [date]. [Describe the
> alternative, e.g. a structured interview.] Requesting one will not count against you.
>
> **Human review.** You can ask [Company] for a human review of your result at [contact].

## 2. General candidate notice (EU, UK and US)

> **About the [job title] assessment**
>
> As part of your application to [Company], you're invited to take a short timed knowledge test on
> **testee**. Please read this before you start.
>
> **What it is.** An AI system that helps us assess candidates; under the EU AI Act it counts as a
> high-risk AI system. [N] multiple-choice questions about [topics], with on average [N] seconds
> for each; each question shows its own timer.
> Questions were written with the help of AI from the job description, [on topics our team chose /
> on topics drawn from it].
> Each candidate gets their own selection of questions. Most have one right option; a "select all
> that apply" question has 2 or 3, and you tick every one you think is right: it earns part of its
> point for each right option ticked, and nothing if a wrong one is ticked. You can change your pick
> until you move to the next question or finish. When a question's time runs out, your pick
> counts; with no pick, it counts as wrong.
>
> **What is recorded.** Your answers (every option ticked), whether each was right or the points
> it earned, how long each took, when you left the test page or copied text, and answers given
> too fast to have read the question, and when you confirmed, before starting, that you'd answer
> on your own. If we give you extra time, that is recorded too, without a reason. We're also shown
> when your answer times or your right and wrong answers don't follow the questions' length and
> difficulty as other candidates' do, with questions to ask you about; it's a reason to ask, never
> a decision. You won't see your score.
>
> [Voice mode only:] **Answering aloud.** The questions and their options are read aloud and you
> can answer aloud. What the microphone picks up while you answer is turned into text by AI and
> matched to the options: you hear and see the option it matched, one clearly named option is
> saved at once and can't then be changed, and on a question with several right options you confirm
> your answer before it counts. testee never stores
> your speech; OpenAI, which turns it into text, may keep it for up to 30 days to check for abuse,
> and doesn't use it for training. Your voice isn't analysed in any other way. You can tap an option
> at any point in the test. Starting needs a working microphone: if you can't use one, or prefer not
> to speak, ask us at [contact] for the test without voice before you start.
>
> **How we use it.** Your result is one part of our assessment, alongside [your CV / interviews].
> A person at [Company] reviews it; no decision is made automatically. You can ask us for a human
> review of your result and for an explanation of how it was used, at [contact].
>
> **Accommodations.** If you need extra time or another adjustment because of a disability or
> another reason, or would prefer a different way to show your knowledge, write to [contact]
> before you start. We can give you more time on every question or arrange an alternative.
> If you apply through our job ad's link, ask us for a personal invite first: extra time can't be
> added there. You don't need to tell us a diagnosis.
>
> **Your data.** [Company] is responsible for your test data; testee (Arcolabs OÜ, Estonia)
> processes it for us. It is kept for 12 months after your invitation and then deleted
> automatically. Our legal basis is [legitimate interest in assessing job knowledge fairly / steps
> before entering into a contract]. Some of it is handled in the United States by testee's
> providers, under the safeguards in testee's data processing agreement (https://testee.ai/dpa).
> You can ask us for a copy of your data, its correction, deletion, restriction or transfer to you
> in a portable form, or object, at [contact]. You can complain to us at [contact] and, in the EU
> or UK, to your data protection authority. testee's privacy policy: https://testee.ai/privacy.
>
> **Emails.** testee sends your invitation and reminders for us. Each has a link to stop our emails
> through testee, or that test's reminders.
>
> **Tips.** Use a computer with a stable connection, in a quiet place, and keep the test page open:
> each question's timer keeps running if you leave.

## 4. Illinois (Human Rights Act, AI in employment)

Add to the general notice for candidates in Illinois, from 2026-01-01:

> **Use of artificial intelligence (Illinois).** [Company] uses artificial intelligence in
> recruitment and hiring for [job title]: testee, a timed knowledge test whose questions and answer
> keys were written with the help of AI and which gives each candidate a grade. We use the grade
> to [decide whom to invite to the next interview / as one part of our assessment]. It doesn't use
> your age, race, sex, disability, zip code or any other protected characteristic, or a proxy for
> one. A person at [Company] reviews every result. Questions, an accommodation or a human review:
> [contact].

## 5. Colorado (SB 26-189, from 2027-01-01)

Before the test, add to the general notice for candidates who live in Colorado:

> **Automated decision-making technology (Colorado).** [Company] uses testee, technology that
> grades a timed knowledge test with answer keys written with the help of AI, and the grade
> [materially influences / is one part of] our decision about [job title]. To learn more about the
> technology, the data it uses and your rights, write to [contact] or see [link].

If the outcome is adverse (not hired, or not invited onward), send within 30 days:

> **About our decision (Colorado).** We decided [not to move your application forward] for
> [job title]. testee's grade of your knowledge test [describe its role, e.g. was below our passing
> grade of N%, which we weighed with your interview]. The system is testee (Arcolabs OÜ, version
> as used on [date]); it used your answers, how long each took and the integrity signals described
> in our notice, all from your test, and your name and email from your application. You can ask us
> for more about the tool, to correct any personal data that is factually wrong, and for a person
> to review the decision, at [contact] by [date].

## 6. Connecticut (Public Act 26-15, from 2027-10-01)

Before the test, add to the general notice for applicants in Connecticut, from 2027-10-01. The
written notice must reach them before the decision is made; sending it before the test does that,
and it also tells them, in plain language, that they will be interacting with the tool.

> **Automated employment-related decision technology (Connecticut).** [Company] has deployed an
> automated employment-related decision technology: **testee**, a timed knowledge test you will
> interact with when you take it. Its purpose is to grade your knowledge of the job, and we use the
> grade [as a substantial factor in / to materially influence] our decision [whether to hire you /
> whether to move your application forward] for [job title].
>
> **What it analyzes and how.** Your answers, with how long each took; when you left the test page
> or copied text; answers given too fast to have read the question; when you confirmed answering
> on your own; any extra time you were given; and your name, email address and sign-in account id.
> Your grade is the percentage of points you earn against answer keys written with the help of AI,
> compared with a passing grade of [N]%. The other data isn't part of the grade: it shows [Company]
> what to ask you about, and never decides on its own.
>
> **Where the data comes from.** From you, in the test, and from your application or your sign-in.
>
> **Contact.** [Company], [address], [contact].
